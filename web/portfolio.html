What the model shows

Improving S3 alone moves the constraint to S2. A second manual S3 fixture increases output, but misses the 240-unit target. The faster S2/S3 case with four production operators and one rework operator reaches demand in 198 of 200 simulated shifts.

Inputs are assumed. The study includes 97 software tests, 11 commissioning cases and 1,200 shifts in the six-configuration cost comparison. Measurement and hardware validation remain necessary before an equipment decision.
